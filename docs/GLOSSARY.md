## Entity vocabulary

- **Frontier:** 19 playable land hexes with ordered edge/corner graph; each terrain supplies one basic resource.
- **District:** Group of three adjacent hexes used for seasonal markets and public contract placement.
- **Season:** Thaw, Bloom, Harvest, Frost; changes every three completed rounds.
- **Contract:** Public delivery objective paying exactly one earned Charter VP, maximum two per player.
- **Reaver Pressure:** Common countdown rising after every full table round; at four it triggers an Invasion.
- **Influence:** Tactical currency earned for participating in defence or fulfilling negotiated deals; never scores as VP.
- **Reaction Token:** One per player per round; gates another player's turn interaction.
- **Reaction Window:** Bounded phase with legal respondent seats; actions resolve in seat order after commitments.
- **Freight Relief:** A once-per-round, 3:1 bank exchange for a player trailing the leader by at least three VP; no free goods or VP.
- **Guarded road:** Road adjacent to an active knight; Bandit tolls and Reaver raids can be resisted.
- **Outpost:** Future expansion concept, not a stage-1 component.

## Strategy card families (design candidates; exact 12/card/deck in Stage 5)

- **Innovation:** Survey Route (move terrain inspection), Seasonal Ledger (forecast production), Efficient Kiln (alter one craft recipe), Relay Wagon (move a Caravan farther), Salvage Plan (recover spent building material), Crop Rotation (convert one terrain yield).
- **Commerce:** Market Bid (raise a delivery payout in goods), Shared Cargo (propose 1:1 trade), Port Commission (improve one bank rate), Double Freight (carry extra delivery), Open Auction (all-seat sealed offers), Credit Note (defer one non-VP payment).
- **Governance:** Call Muster (ready knight), Shield Wall (temporary defence), Right of Way (override a toll), Truce Charter (prevent blockade), Council Vote (alter district demand), Night Watch (protect stores).

These are thematic groups, not legal exact card effects yet. No player may gain an unearned VP directly from drawing a Strategy Card.

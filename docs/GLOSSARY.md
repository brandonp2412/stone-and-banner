# Original terminology

Stone & Banner is its own world. Village → Town → Capital; Reaver Fleet → Invasion; Bandit disrupts roads; Caravan trades; Knight defends; Strategy Cards are Innovation (green), Commerce (yellow) and Governance (blue); Town Developments open economic tiers; Guardian's Honour rewards defence. Goods: wood, brick, wheat, wool, ore, paper, cloth and coin. Do not use source-game branding, text, visuals or code.

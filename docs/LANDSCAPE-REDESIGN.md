# Landscape tabletop redesign — 15 November 2026

The mobile app now starts in immersive landscape, supporting both landscape
orientations. A match stays on one table, with public player standings at the
top, the map in the centre, private inventory below, and Build / Trade / Cards /
Realm controls at the right. The map, inventory and primary turn action remain
in place while panels change or scroll.

The terrain drawings, perspective projection, production tokens and coastal
contours are original Flutter canvas work. Production numbers have probability
pips; the last dice total and matching production hexes remain visible. All
painting, pointer targets and accessible target lists share the same projection.
The terrain decorations do not advance randomness. Rules and replay reducers
under `lib/engine/` were not changed.

Home and match setup use landscape layouts. Trades and card choices open in
focused dialogs. Short phones use compact controls, including a single row of
construction choices and two immediately visible trade entry points. Replays
remain importable with the keyboard open; autocorrect and suggestions are off.
The public board remains visible during computer turns and tactical responses,
with private hands concealed. Opening the match menu pauses computer work.

Layout references were the spatial arrangement of the official
[CATAN Universe screenshots](https://www.catan.com/catan-universe).
No reference artwork, game text or assets were copied into the app.

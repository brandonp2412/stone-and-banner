# Originality and publication gate

This is an independent strategy design. The familiar general ideas of hex grids, trade, roads, settlements and Victory Points are mechanics—not a licence to reproduce another game's protected presentation.

- No CATAN branding, publisher logos, licensed artwork, iconography, text, card names, exact rules prose, character names, sounds, trade dress or promotional comparison in the app.
- Commission/design wholly original landscapes, board pieces, symbols, UI and copy. Track asset provenance and licence for every imported asset.
- Preserve differentiators in the final rules: seasonal demand and craft chains, two-action economy, road tolling Caravan trade, seat-order reaction commitments, and earned bounded catch-up procurement.
- Design proposal needs independent playtesting and legal review for trademark/copyright/trade-dress risk and board-game rule similarities before any public store listing, beta marketing, monetization or distribution.
- Stage-1 APK is for an authorised private device trial only. Glass currently overrides signing with its managed certificate. This is not a public release.

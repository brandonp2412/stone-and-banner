## Important limits

- The live one-human/two-AI game was played through opening draft, dice and one full AI turn cycle, then saved/resumed at Round 2. It was **not** played to 15 VP.
- The observed 15/15 VP win was a separate deterministic 1,143-event pre-win replay, imported into an actual Linux release executable, then continued by a manual legal Capital upgrade. It is **not** a natural AI victory.
- Waydroid Android UI had user 11 running another Stone & Banner session. No switch, install or tap was made. Screenshots are Linux only.
- LAN was verified with a fresh three-socket protocol test, not independent host/guest GUI applications this slice.
- The long replay needed slower keyboard injection; the first fast-typed version produced an Invalid replay toast. No app import defect established.
- Victory screen does not expose an in-place Rematch button in the observed viewport. Needs product decision and a dedicated GUI retest.

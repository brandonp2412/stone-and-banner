## Coverage and limits

- **Real Linux release interactions:** Home, replay import, 89-event replay restoration, private handoff, match board, Strategy hand, Founding Charter detail, target selection, discard-confirmation cancellation, explicit play and one-time goods transfer. Two full card-play loops were screenshot-verified.
- **Automated physical-card matrix:** all 54 cards (18 per deck), 352 legal target/exchange choices in seeded state, every choice reducer-accepted with stock/copy accounting and duplicate rejection. Separate zero-stock and Governance reaction tests. This is deterministic engine proof, not 54 live-screen captures.
- **Live card-specific screenshot coverage:** Founding Charter only (1 of 54 physical copies). All other card-specific UI flows remain unverified on actual Linux/Waydroid, despite pure-Dart legality and widget tests.
- **Waydroid:** no app screen opened and no UI action performed in this slice; no Waydroid screenshots. Nox Waydroid user 10 was already active for Slice 12 and was deliberately not switched or overwritten. Glass execution connector returned HTTP 429 twice.
- **UI finding:** normal match action toolbar remains dense on a 390×844 portrait screen. The one-choice card sheet was shortened and re-screenshoted (#12 → #23).
- **No Samsung, physical-device installation, hosted service, or public release.**

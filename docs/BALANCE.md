# Balance hypotheses to test

Target viability of Innovation, Commerce and Governance across seed/turn order. Measure first-seat wins, VP-deficit comebacks, turns per match, dead rounds, reaction usage and goods scarcity with seeded, replayable simulations. Compare interventions against unmodified rules. The bounded once-per-round 3:1 bank trade improves recovery without awarding free VP. Do not call any deck balanced without measured evidence.
